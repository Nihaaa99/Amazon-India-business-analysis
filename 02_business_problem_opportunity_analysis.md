# Amazon India — Business Problem & Opportunity Analysis

**Version:** 1.0 | **Prepared by:** Independent Business Analyst

## Background and objective
I selected Amazon India to understand an e-commerce website from a customer's point of view. I explored product search, selection, cart management, checkout, payment, order tracking and returns. The aim is to understand the current process and identify possible customer-experience improvements. This is an independent case study, not an official Amazon project.

## Observed strengths
| Area | Observation | Possible customer benefit |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Personalised recommendations and browsing history | Easier product discovery |
| Search | Multiple filters and sorting options | More targeted searches |
| Product page | Detailed descriptions and customer reviews | Better-informed decisions |
| Pricing | Discounts, offers and price history | Price transparency |
| Cart | Easy quantity updates and removal | Convenient cart management |
| Checkout | Detailed charges and savings | Clearer payable amount |
| Delivery | Estimated dates and delivery options | Better expectations |
| Tracking | Multiple shipment stages | Order progress visibility |
| Support | Return, replacement and support options | Post-purchase assistance |

## Potential opportunities
These are areas to investigate, not confirmed defects or known internal Amazon problems.

### Product discovery
**Observation:** Many filters, sorting options and recommendations are available.  
**Opportunity:** Check whether selected filters and product comparisons could be easier to review.

### Product evaluation
**Observation:** Product pages contain descriptions, reviews, offers, price history, variants and recommendations.  
**Opportunity:** Consider a clearer summary of important product information to support comparison.

### Shopping cart
**Observation:** Quantity updates and removal were easy. Save for Later was not located.  
**Opportunity:** Further investigate the visibility of saved-item functionality.

### Delivery
**Observation:** Delivery options, estimated dates, savings and applicable charges were displayed.  
**Opportunity:** Investigate whether delivery alternatives and costs are easy to compare.

### Tracking
**Observation:** Shipment progress and estimated delivery dates were visible. No notifications were visible on the page during exploration.  
**Opportunity:** Check proactive notifications and changes to delivery estimates.

### Returns and support
**Observation:** Return and replacement options were available, but exact eligibility could not be verified for older orders.  
**Opportunity:** Check how clearly eligibility, deadlines and refund information are explained.

## Opportunity assessment
| Opportunity | Evidence | Further validation |
|---|---|---|
| Product comparison | Potential opportunity | Customer research |
| Save for Later visibility | Not located | Further interface investigation |
| Delivery comparison | Options observed | Compare delivery scenarios |
| Tracking notifications | Not seen on page | Check email, SMS and app |
| Return eligibility clarity | Partially observed | Check product categories |
| Refund information | Reported as clear | Validate refund methods |

## Expected benefits
If validated and implemented, possible benefits include improved shopping convenience, product evaluation, purchase decisions, delivery transparency, order-status awareness and post-purchase support. These are expected benefits, not measured outcomes.

## Limitations
No internal Amazon documents, operational data, customer surveys, employee interviews or customer analytics were accessed. Features may vary by account, location, product and date. Proposed improvements have not been validated with Amazon stakeholders.

**Conclusion:** The identified opportunities are starting points for further analysis and should be validated before being treated as confirmed business requirements.
