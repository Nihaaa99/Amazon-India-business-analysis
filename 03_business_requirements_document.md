# Business Requirements Document (BRD)
## Amazon India — Online Shopping Experience

**Version:** 1.0 — Draft  
**Prepared by:** Independent Business Analyst

## 1. Introduction
I selected Amazon India to understand how an e-commerce website works from a customer's point of view. I explored product search and browsing, product selection, cart management, checkout, payment, order tracking and returns. The purpose is to understand the current shopping process and identify possible improvements. This is an independent analysis, not an official Amazon project.

## 2. Objectives
- Understand how customers search for and select products.
- Study product information and reviews.
- Understand cart and checkout.
- Examine delivery and payment options.
- Understand order tracking and support.
- Identify possible improvements.

## 3. Scope
**Included:** Homepage and navigation; product search and filters; product details and reviews; cart; delivery address; delivery options and charges; payment methods; order confirmation and tracking; returns and customer support.

**Excluded:** Internal Amazon operations, warehouse and inventory management, seller-side operations, internal payment gateway systems and courier operations.

## 4. Stakeholders
| Stakeholder | Involvement |
|---|---|
| Customer | Searches, selects and purchases products |
| Seller | Provides products for sale |
| Amazon platform | Provides shopping and order-management experience |
| Delivery partner | Delivers the product |
| Payment service provider | Processes customer payments |
| Customer support team | Helps with order-related problems |

These roles are based on the visible customer journey. Internal responsibilities have not been verified.

## 5. Business requirements
| ID | Requirement |
|---|---|
| BR-01 | Customers should be able to search for products and browse categories. |
| BR-02 | Customers should be able to filter and sort products. |
| BR-03 | Customers should be able to view product descriptions, prices, ratings and reviews. |
| BR-04 | Customers should be able to add products to the cart and manage quantities. |
| BR-05 | Customers should be able to select or update their delivery address. |
| BR-06 | Customers should be able to view delivery options, estimated dates and applicable charges. |
| BR-07 | Customers should be able to review the complete order amount before payment. |
| BR-08 | Customers should be able to choose from payment methods available to them. |
| BR-09 | Customers should receive order confirmation after successful ordering. |
| BR-10 | Customers should be able to track their orders. |
| BR-11 | Customers should be able to access return and replacement options when eligible. |
| BR-12 | Customers should be able to contact customer support for order-related problems. |

These describe customer needs for this study; they are not Amazon-approved internal requirements.

## 6. Business rules
1. Customers select a delivery address before confirming delivery availability.
2. Delivery options and charges may depend on product and location.
3. The final payable amount should be visible before payment.
4. Payment options may depend on eligibility and order.
5. Order tracking becomes available after an order is placed.
6. Return and replacement options depend on product eligibility and applicable conditions.

Exact rules require further verification.

## 7. Functional requirements summary
The website should support product search, filtering, sorting, product information and reviews, cart management, address selection, delivery options, order summary, payment selection, order confirmation, tracking, returns and support. See the functional and non-functional requirements document.

## 8. Non-functional requirements summary
The website should be easy to navigate, respond within an acceptable time, protect customer and payment information, remain reliable, work across supported devices and browsers, display clear pricing and support accessibility. These are proposed expectations, not measured findings.

## 9. Success criteria
For this case study, the journey is covered when a customer can find and select a product, manage it in the cart, review delivery and charges, select an available payment option, access confirmation and tracking, and find return or support options when eligible.

## 10. Assumptions and limitations
Features may vary by location, account and product. No internal Amazon documents or data were accessed and no employee/customer interviews were conducted. Return, refund and payment rules have not been fully verified. Requirements are independently prepared for learning and portfolio use.

## 11. Conclusion
This document captures the main customer requirements identified during the exploration. They will be used to prepare user stories, acceptance criteria and test cases.
