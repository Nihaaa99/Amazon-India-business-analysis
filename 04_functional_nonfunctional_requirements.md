# Functional and Non-Functional Requirements
## Amazon India — Online Shopping Experience

**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Introduction
This document lists requirements identified from my exploration of Amazon India's website. I observed product search, product information, cart management, checkout, payment and order tracking. These requirements describe the functions and quality expectations of the shopping platform.

## 2. Functional requirements

### Homepage and product discovery
| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-01 | Customers can browse product categories. | High |
| FR-02 | Customers can view personalised recommendations. | Medium |
| FR-03 | Customers can access browsing and search history. | Medium |
| FR-04 | Customers can view delivery-location information. | High |

### Search and filtering
| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| FR-05 | Customers can search using the search bar. | High |

| FR-07 | Customers can sort search results. | High |
| FR-08 | Search results display product name, brand, price, ratings and estimated delivery. | High |
| FR-09 | Sponsored listings are identifiable. | Medium |

## 3. Non-functional requirements
These are proposed quality expectations, not performance measurements from the exploration.

| ID | Requirement | Category |
|---|---|---|
| NFR-01 | Website is easy to navigate across shopping stages. | Usability |
| NFR-02 | Search results and product pages load within an acceptable time. | Performance |
| NFR-03 | Customer and payment information is protected. | Security |
| NFR-04 | Shopping services remain available and reliable. | Availability |
| NFR-05 | Website works across supported browsers and devices. | Compatibility |
| NFR-06 | Product prices, delivery charges and order totals are displayed clearly. | Accuracy |
| NFR-07 | Important shopping functions support accessibility needs. | Accessibility |

## 4. Priorities
**High:** Important for completing the main shopping process.  
**Medium:** Useful functionality that supports the customer experience.

Priorities are proposed for this case study and have not been approved by Amazon.

## 5. Conclusion
These requirements cover the main activities observed while shopping on Amazon India. They form the basis for user stories, acceptance criteria and test cases.
