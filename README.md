# Amazon India — End-to-End Shopping Journey Analysis

An independent Business Analyst portfolio case study based on a hands-on exploration of the customer-facing Amazon India website.

> This is an independent learning project, not affiliated with or approved by Amazon. Findings describe the experience observed during exploration and do not represent Amazon's internal processes or requirements.

## Objective
Understand the customer journey from product discovery through checkout, order tracking and post-purchase support. Document the current process, identify possible improvement areas, and prepare requirements and test designs.

## Research stages
1. Homepage and navigation
2. Product search and filtering
3. Product details and reviews
4. Shopping cart
5. Checkout and payment options
6. Order confirmation and tracking
7. Returns and customer support

## Project files
- 01_as_is_process_flow.md — current customer journey
- 02_business_problem_opportunity_analysis.md — observations and possible improvements
- 03_business_requirements_document.md — scope and business requirements

- 06_to_be_process_flow.md — proposed journey

- 08_test_cases.csv — manual test case design

## Testing status
Test cases are designed but have not been executed as a formal test cycle. Do not mark a test Pass without actual execution and evidence. No internal Amazon data, stakeholder interviews, customer analytics or system architecture were accessed.

## Methods
Manual website exploration, process mapping, requirements documentation, user stories, traceability and manual test design.
