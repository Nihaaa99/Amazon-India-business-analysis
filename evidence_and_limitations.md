# Evidence, Assumptions and Limitations

## Directly observed during website exploration
- Homepage navigation, categories and recommendations.
- Search history and product recommendations.
- Search filters and sorting options for wireless mouse results.
- Product cards showing brand, name, price, delivery estimate and reviews.
- Sponsored listings.
- Product detail information, pricing, reviews, variants and purchase actions.
- Frequently bought together recommendations.
- Cart quantity management, product removal, subtotal and recommendations.
- Address selection and update options.
- Delivery options, charges and order summary.
- Payment method options displayed before payment submission.
- Order information and shipment tracking details.
- Return and customer support options where accessible.

## Based on the researcher's previous experience
- Authenticated payment experience and perceived payment security.
- Some delivery and post-purchase interactions.
- Some refund and replacement experience.

## Not verified or only partially verified
- Exact return eligibility for different products.
- Exact return deadlines and exceptions.
- Exact pickup address change conditions.
- Payment failure and retry behaviour.
- Notification delivery through email, SMS or app.
- Current availability and exact timing of Amazon Now.
- Internal business rules, system architecture or operational workflows.
- Performance, security and accessibility measurements.

## Assumptions used in documentation
- The process represents a typical customer journey, not every possible path.
- Product, delivery and payment options can vary.
- Business requirements are independently proposed customer needs, not Amazon-approved requirements.
- TO-BE changes are ideas for further investigation, not confirmed solutions.

## Testing status
The test cases in this repository are designed but not executed as a formal test cycle. Do not mark any case as Pass or Fail until it has actually been performed and evidence has been recorded.
