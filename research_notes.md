# Research Notes — Amazon India Website Exploration

These notes summarise observations supplied during the hands-on website exploration. They are not a full audit and may vary by account, location, product and date.

## 1. Homepage
- Header included search, delivery address, All/categories, customer service and browsing history.
- Categories included shop by category, digital content and devices, best sellers and new products.
- Search showed previous search history and recommended products.
- Recommendations included previously viewed or purchased items and Buy Again products.
- Delivery address and estimated delivery date were visible.

## 2. Product search — wireless mouse
- Filters observed included customer reviews, discount, price range, features, availability, length and purpose.
- Sorting options were extensive and allowed results to be adjusted to the intended use of the mouse.
- Product cards displayed brand, product name, sale price, estimated delivery and reviews.
- Delivery estimates and Prime-related free delivery information were visible.
- Sponsored products were present.
- The researcher found search easy to navigate.

## 3. Product details
- Product page showed details, colour and characteristics.
- Offers and an Ask Alexa/product questions area were observed.
- A section showed how many people bought the product over recent months.
- Original price, discounts, offers and price history were visible.
- Ratings, written reviews, image reviews and helpful votes were available.
- Product variants were available.
- Wishlist, Add to Cart and Buy Now actions were visible.
- Frequently bought together recommendations showed combined pricing and an add-both-to-cart option.
- Product and seller information were displayed.
- Warranty, delivery, replacement and payment information were available on the page.

## 4. Shopping cart
- Product details and main navigation options were visible.
- Quantity could be increased or decreased.
- Product removal appeared to respond within approximately 2–3 seconds, based on the researcher's observation.
- Save for Later was not located during this exploration.
- Subtotal and applicable charges were displayed.
- Recommendations included products often bought with the selected mouse and products previously searched.
- Proceed to Buy was highlighted.
- An Amazon Now route for eligible fast delivery was noticed, described as approximately 20–30 minutes. Availability and timing were not independently verified.

## 5. Checkout and payment
- Saved address options could be selected and delivery location could be updated.
- Delivery options showed offers, savings, free delivery where applicable and handling fees.
- Charges were described as precisely displayed.
- Order summary showed original price, savings and current payable amount.
- Payment options appeared after confirming/updating location.
- Card, UPI and Pay Later options were observed.
- The researcher described the payment flow as authenticated based on previous experience.
- Navigation and reviewing details were described as easy.

## 6. Order confirmation and tracking
- Confirmation area included tracking and a product summary.
- Product details were available in order details.
- Payment status could be viewed for Pay Now or Pay Later.
- Shipment tracking showed approximately four to five stages.
- Delivery estimates varied by Prime or Amazon Now in the researcher's experience.
- No notifications were visible on the page during the exploration.
- Cancel, replace or complaint/help routes were described as available through customer support.

## 7. Returns and customer support
- Exact eligibility could not be checked for older purchases because the researcher had not bought anything during the previous year.
- The researcher recalled return windows of about one or two weeks, but this was not verified for a particular item.
- Around four or five return reason options were observed or recalled.
- Refund information was described as easy to understand.
- Replacement was available in the explored context.
- Pickup address selection appeared to offer the delivered address and possibly another address; this needs validation.
- More complex problems could be connected to a customer support agent.
- A next step or status appeared after confirming return or replacement, according to the researcher's observation.

## Research limitations
- No new order was placed for this project.
- No payment was submitted.
- Exact return eligibility and deadlines were not verified against a current order.
- No internal Amazon documents, system data, stakeholder interviews or customer analytics were accessed.
- Observations may change over time and vary by account, location and product.
